How to Play

🎟️ Getting a Ticket

Each ticket has 3 rows and 9 columns with 15 numbers (5 per row) from 1–90. Tap "Get My Ticket" and pick a number.

🎙️ For the Host

  • Open the app and tap "I'm the Host"
  • Tap "Call Next Number" to draw a random number
  • Announce it loudly to all players
  • Keep calling until someone wins

✅ Marking Numbers

When the host calls a number on your ticket, tap it to mark it green.

🏆 Winning

  • Early Five — first 5 numbers marked
  • Line — all 5 in any row marked
  • Full House — all 15 numbers marked

Shout "HOUSIE!" when you win!
